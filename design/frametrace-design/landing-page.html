01 · Capture

Press F10. Play. Press it again.

No overlay, no injection, no per-game setup. Frametrace runs Intel PresentMon in the background and records every frame of whatever game you are playing.

  • Fixed length from 5 seconds to an hour, or stop it yourself
  • Finds the game from the foreground window when you press the key
  • Beeps on start and stop, so you never alt-tab to check

02 · Verdict

It tells you what is limiting you.

Frametrace compares how long the GPU was busy with how long each frame took. 93% GPU-limited means a faster CPU buys you nothing, and a lower render resolution buys you a lot.

  • GPU-limited, CPU-limited, or held by a cap or VSync
  • Hitches judged against their neighbours, not a global average
  • PC latency from NVIDIA Reflex markers, median and P95

03 · Report

One block of text. Any AI can read it.

The report holds the numbers, your hardware, the display path and the checks worth fixing first. It is copied the moment a capture ends. Paste it into Claude, ChatGPT or the Frametrace skill and ask what to change.

  • Checks EXPO, single-channel RAM, HAGS, Game Mode, power plan
  • Compares two captures line by line
  • Short enough for any chat window
